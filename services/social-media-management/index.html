USEFUL TO KNOW

Before we get started.

Does this include social media graphics?

Graphics are one of ARSC’s primary capabilities and can be discussed alongside management. The number, formats and responsibilities are agreed in scope rather than assumed as part of a fixed package.

What does reporting look like?

The homepage includes three reporting design previews for different client contexts. They contain clearly labelled fictional figures, not live account data or performance results.

Can I start with one priority?

ARSC offers project or ongoing support shaped around your goals and priorities. Discuss the work you need first; the right level of support depends on the scope and capacity involved.

Who approves and publishes the content?

Agree the channels, content approvals, publishing responsibilities and account access before work begins. If you need comment or inbox support too, raise that in your enquiry so it can be discussed explicitly rather than assumed.

How are cost and timing agreed?

Share your channels, content needs, campaign dates and available source material. We discuss the deliverables, capacity, cost and schedule before you decide to proceed. No fixed posting frequency or growth guarantee is promised here.
