FROM FIRST HELLO TO FINAL TOUCH

A little planning.
A much calmer morning.

  1. Tell me about the day.

    Share your date, location, ready-by time and the number of people. A photo of your outfit or a few references helps, but you don’t need a perfectly curated mood board.

  2. Find the right finish.

    For bridal appointments, a trial gives us time to explore texture, colour and wear. Take photos in different light and notice how the look feels over the next few hours.

  3. Make room for the moment.

    We agree a running order and set aside a bright, tidy workspace. On the day, there’s a little breathing room at the end for touch-ups and a final check before you leave.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Come as you are.
Bring a little context.

Your skin, your comfort.

Arrive with a clean face if you can and follow your usual, familiar routine. There’s no need to try a new treatment just before an event. Tell the artist about sensitivities, allergies or products you prefer not to use; a live service would confirm suitability with you.

A thoughtful kit.

The imagined studio protocol uses cleaned tools, disposable applicators where appropriate and decanted products. If you’d rather use your own mascara or lip colour, bring it along. Comfortable boundaries are part of good service.

A little room to work.

For an on-location appointment, a table near daylight, an accessible power point and a clear chair make all the difference. Wear something that opens at the front so changing clothes doesn’t disturb the finished look.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before we meet.

Can you work with different skin tones and textures?

That’s the intention of this studio concept: a varied complexion kit, undertone matching in daylight and a conversation about coverage. The fictional imagery shows different people, not a claim of real client experience. A working artist should discuss your needs and show relevant, genuine examples.

Do I need a bridal trial?

It isn’t included in the base bridal rate. A trial is useful if you’re unsure about the look, trying lashes for the first time or want to see how makeup photographs. The preview lets you add one trial to your estimate for $110.

Can you come to our getting-ready location?

The imagined service covers Brisbane and nearby locations by arrangement. Travel time, parking and access would be confirmed in a written quote; they aren’t calculated by this demo. There is no real address or available appointment behind this page.

What happens if plans change?

A real booking would have written rescheduling and cancellation terms before any deposit is paid. This portfolio does not collect deposits or confirm appointments. You can change the selections below as often as you like without creating a booking.
