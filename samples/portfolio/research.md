## Photography and cropping decisions

The earlier gallery used shallow fixed-height landscape strips, which cropped the top and bottom of images even with adjusted focal positions. Gallery previews and the full photography/café pages now preserve source aspect ratios. Product images also preserve their original framing, while lightboxes use `object-fit: contain`.

Fifteen new assets add a variety of adult people and separate garment/component photos. New prompts deliberately reserve headroom and full-body margins where appropriate. Existing photos are not reused as unrelated section filler. Hero thumbnails and selected-product/photo viewers intentionally show the asset associated with that item.

## Functional scope

All pages work as classic-script static sites, including opening the HTML files directly. Shopping bags are in-memory only, separate size variants, enforce a ten-per-option demo limit, allow quantity removal and calculate delivery thresholds in integer cents. Order summaries stop before payment. Enquiry and repair forms never send or retain input; native `method="dialog"` also prevents default HTTP submission if scripts are unavailable. The older interior, accommodation and makeup concepts remain at their existing URLs.
