OUR POINT OF VIEW / NOT MORE. MORE USEFUL.

Some clothes make an entrance.
These make themselves at home.

OFFDAY is the label we imagined for the hours without a dress code. A coffee before the train. A long lunch that becomes a walk. The quiet pleasure of wearing something that doesn’t need your attention.

In our fictional story, founders Nina and Sami met while sharing a cramped design studio. They kept returning to the same problem: their most-worn clothes were rarely the loudest ones. The first edit began with four familiar silhouettes, adjusted until they felt easy rather than oversized for its own sake.

The palette is deliberately small—navy, ecru, sand and olive. The shapes leave room to move. Details earn their place: a pocket that holds a phone, a shirt hem that works untucked, a waistband that doesn’t make sitting down an event.

This is an original portfolio brand, not a claim about a real manufacturer, supply chain or clothing collection. The materials below are the intended specifications of the fictional garments.

GET THE FIT RIGHT / CONCEPT MEASUREMENTS IN CM

A little room.
A better starting point.

The collection uses relaxed silhouettes, but relaxed doesn’t mean the same fit on every body. Compare the chart with your own measurements, then check the notes for the individual piece.

The figures below are illustrative body measurements, not garment measurements. Measure chest and hips around their fullest points and waist at your natural waistline. For the trousers, the concept inseam is 76 cm across sizes. A real product page would confirm finished garment dimensions and tolerances.

The shirt and overshirt are boxy; the tee is a relaxed straight cut; the trousers sit at the natural waist with room through the leg. If you’re between sizes, think about how much ease you prefer rather than treating a label as a judgement.

LOOK AFTER THE THINGS YOU LIKE

Wear. Air. Repeat.

Cotton jersey & poplin.

The intended care instructions for the tee and shirt are a cool, gentle wash with similar colours, reshaping while damp and drying in the shade. Turn the navy shirt inside out. A cool iron can help the poplin; the tee doesn’t need a perfectly pressed finish.

Cotton–linen.

A little creasing belongs to the Easy Trouser’s imagined texture. Wash gently at 30°C, avoid tumble drying and hang from the waistband. Steam or iron while slightly damp if you prefer a cleaner line.

Cotton twill.

Air the overshirt between wears and spot-clean small marks when appropriate. The intended label calls for a cool gentle wash, no bleach and shade drying. These are concept instructions; a real garment’s sewn-in label always comes first.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you add it.

Are these real products I can buy?

No. OFFDAY is a fictional ARSC website concept. The photographs are generated; materials, measurements and prices illustrate a realistic store experience. The bag works locally, but there is no real checkout or inventory.

How does the sizing work?

Each piece offers XS–XL in the pictured colour. Use the body-size chart and the individual fit note as the starting point. There is no automatic fit recommendation, because this demo cannot know how a real garment would fit you.

Will the bag remember my selection?

Only while this page stays open. Reloading starts a fresh bag. No personal information, product selection or payment information is sent or retained.

Do you claim certified or sustainable materials?

No. The fabric descriptions are fictional design specifications. This concept makes no verified certification, ethical-production or environmental-performance claims.
