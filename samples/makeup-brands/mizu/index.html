A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

Keep it simple.

Do I need a brush?

No. Warm a small amount between fingers, press into skin and blend the edges. A brush is optional.

Can I build the coverage?

Yes. Allow the first layer to settle, then press a little more only where you want it. Water Veil is designed around sheer coverage.

How does the routine builder work?

Choose a base and a finish. The builder combines your selections, shows the total and adds them to your demo bag.
