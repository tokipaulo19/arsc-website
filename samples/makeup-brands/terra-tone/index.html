FIELD NOTES / 001

Undertone is
not an afterthought.

Depth is only the beginning. A shade can be exactly light enough or deep enough, and still feel a little wrong. Undertone is the quiet colour beneath the surface.

Warm and cool: a starting point

Golden and peach tones often sit in the warm family. Pink or red tones often sit in the cool family. Neutral skin may contain both.

When should I test a shade?

Try a small swatch along the jaw and check it in daylight after it has settled. Compare the face and neck rather than the hand.

Less coverage, more flexibility

Start with a thin layer and build where you want it. A sheer tint lets the natural variation of your skin show through.
