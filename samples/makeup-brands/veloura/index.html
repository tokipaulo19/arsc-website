INSIDE THE MAISON

private ceremony.

There is a moment between the last button and the first step outside. Veloura belongs to that moment. Satin pigment, lacquered objects and colour that feels like part of your wardrobe.

The philosophy of the edit +

Choose fewer things, more deliberately. Our evening edit begins with a single defining colour, then builds texture around it: satin, softness and shine.

How to wear Rouge Élan +

Apply directly for a full satin lip, or press on with a fingertip for a diffused stain. Add Laque Nocturne at the centre to catch the light.
